How It Works

Read. Score. Improve.

A complete reading loop — from passage to personalized feedback in under 2 minutes.

1 📖

Pick a passage

Teacher-curated library with grade-level classification powered by IBM Granite

2 🎙️

Read out loud

Azure Speech scores accuracy, fluency, and completeness word by word in real time

3 🤖

Get AI feedback

IBM Granite writes warm, personalized feedback calibrated to the student's score level

4 📚

Practice hard words

Animated syllable cards with definitions help students decode exactly what tripped them up

Features

Built for struggling readers

Every feature is designed around one goal — helping kids who are behind catch up faster.

🎯

Pronunciation Scoring

Azure Speech SDK scores every word for accuracy, fluency, and completeness — not just overall performance.

Azure Speech
💬

Personalized AI Feedback

IBM Granite writes warm, age-appropriate feedback with 5 tiers — from celebrating a 95% to keeping a 30% student from giving up.

IBM Granite 4
📊

Phonics Pattern Groups

Mispronounced words are grouped by phonics pattern — so students learn the rule, not just the word.

Unique Feature
🐛

Bookworm AI Tutor

A conversational reading tutor that asks comprehension questions, drills tough words, and tracks how confident students feel.

IBM Granite 4
🇲🇽

Spanish Language Support

Spanish-speaking students read English passages and receive all feedback in Spanish — so language isn't a barrier to learning.

Detroit-Focused
🔤

Syllable Animation

Word cards break mispronounced words into syllables that light up one by one as the word is spoken slowly.
